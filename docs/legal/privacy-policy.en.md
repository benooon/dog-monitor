# Dog Monitor privacy policy

Last updated: [date of publication]

Dog Monitor turns a spare iPhone into a camera for your dog. This page says what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

**Who we are.** [Legal name or owner name], [address], Israel. Contact: [privacy email].

## What we collect

- **Your account.** When you sign in with Apple we get an Apple user ID and, if you share it, your name. We do not ask for or store your email.
- **Your household.** Its name, members, dogs (name, photo, a few answers about your dog) and the phones you add (name, model, app version, notification token, battery level, time zone).
- **What the camera notices.** Events such as barking or "at the door", the dog's state, a short clip and a snapshot, and a daily summary. The analysis runs on the camera phone. Clips include sound when you allow the microphone.
- **Live view.** When someone in your household watches live, video and sound pass through our live-video provider. We do not save the live stream.
- **Purchases.** Your plan and when it renews. Apple handles payment; we never see your card.
- **Technical logs.** Our server records the address of each request, its time and any errors.

We do not use advertising or analytics tools, we do not track you across other apps, and we do not sell your data.

## Why we use it

To run the app: sign you in, show your household its camera and events, send notifications, and provide Premium. Our legal bases (for people in the EU and UK) are performing our contract with you and, for optional features such as sharing clips to improve detection, your consent. Keeping the service secure is our legitimate interest.

- Clips and snapshots: 7 days. If the household owner chooses "help improve detection", clips you mark right or wrong are kept up to 90 days.
- Everything else: until you delete your account. [Events and daily summaries will get an automatic limit.]
- Server logs: [period set by our hosting provider].

## Your choices and rights

- **Delete your account** in the app (Settings → Account). This removes your sign-in, your phones and, if you are the only member, your household with all its events, clips and snapshots. If other people are in the household, remove them first.
- **See, correct or export your data**, or object to processing: write to [privacy email]. We answer within 30 days.
- **Cancel Premium** in your Apple ID subscriptions. Deleting the account does not cancel it.
- If you are in the EU or UK you can complain to your data protection authority. In Israel, to the Privacy Protection Authority.
- Notifications and microphone access can be turned off in iOS Settings.

## People the camera can see and hear

If you point a camera where guests, a dog walker or a cleaner may be, tell them. The camera screen shows when someone is watching or talking. Some countries require consent before recording sound of other people.

## Children

Dog Monitor is for adults. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Security

Connections are encrypted in transit. Live video is not end-to-end encrypted. We limit access to our systems, but no system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

## Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will tell you in the app before it applies.
